=====
V4110
=====

.. problem-summary:: V4110

The pair link's measured timing left the envelope recorded by the
commissioning calibration: a ping/pong round trip or its EMA10 rose
above the calibrated maximum on either direction
(HA_PERFORMANCE_DEGRADED, ADR-0062). Redundancy keeps working — this
alarm surfaces the drift (cable aging, added adapters, load growth)
while it is recoverable, instead of silently eroding the failover
guarantee. Failover thresholds are never automatically retuned.

Example
-------

A pair commissioned with a 2-tick calibrated round trip sees its link
degrade to 10 ticks:

.. code-block::

   Error: V4110 - performance degraded: the measured pair-link timing left the calibrated envelope: HA_PERFORMANCE_DEGRADED; investigate the link and recalibrate the pair

To fix this alarm, inspect the pair link: the medium (cable aging, a
replaced NIC or adapter), the link speed, and the load on both units.
Once the cause is repaired, run a new commissioning calibration — a
recalibration re-freezes the envelope and clears the alarm.
