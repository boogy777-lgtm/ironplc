=====
V4106
=====

.. problem-summary:: V4106

A fencing claim was rejected: a live originator still holds the module's
exclusive ownership at the I/O target. This is the partition evidence —
a primary that is alive behind a network partition still owns its
outputs, so the target's exclusivity decides in favor of the living
owner and the claimant's ownership barrier retreats. The barrier
releases everything it had already acquired and the unit lands in
REDUNDANCY_LOST: the pair is no longer redundant, and recovery is a
manual repair, never a retry.

Example
-------

A secondary, promoted by the proven-death path after a partition,
attempts its takeover claim while the old primary is in fact alive and
still owning:

.. code-block::

   Error: V4106 - owner conflict: a claim was rejected: a live owner still holds the module's exclusive ownership

To fix this error, repair the partition (check the pair-link wiring and
the daisy-chain path), then requalify the unit out of REDUNDANCY_LOST.
A claim that the target rejects is the fence working as designed — the
alternative, two owners of one output, is what the barrier exists to
prevent.
